# Listening review, 2026-10-07 (the first)

Every clip saved since the ears shipped (10-3 to 10-7): 194, of which 184 loved, 5 grated and 5 snapshots.
33 carry a Gemini critique (the other 161 hit the free quota's 429s). Sources: `node ear/review.mjs`
(numbers, below), the five grated clips' spectrograms set against loved ones, the 33 critiques, and
the code that made the sounds. How a review is run: [README.md](README.md).

## Corrections and what came of it (10-8)

Two numbers below were wrong, and building the fixes showed why:

- **Far shore isn't quiet.** Its loves are mostly on SuperCollider, which pulled the cross-engine median
  down. Within each engine Far shore sits within a dB of Deep field; what holds everywhere is Afterhours
  about 2.5 dB over the rest and Blue hour about 4 under (4 clips).
- **SuperCollider is about 2 dB under Sampled, not 4.** The browser engines are recorded at the limiter,
  before your Volume; SuperCollider records after its level. `ear/review.mjs` now reports the level you
  heard (browser clips get the Volume and the mood's trim added back), and clips record the trim.

Built from this review (0.14.0), with Alex's pick of proposals 1-3: SuperCollider's kick and bass
(offline renders of Afterhours with the real script: sub 58% to 28%, the same peak, 4.6 dB less energy,
the bass ducking under the kick), mood trims (Afterhours -2.5 dB, Blue hour +2), and Afterhours' kick
resting the last bar of every eight with softer, varied open hats. Proposals 4-6 wait for Alex.

## What you love

- **Deep field, on SuperCollider.** 82 of the 184 loves are Deep field, 62 of those on SuperCollider.
  Gemini agrees: the best received of anything, "all-day wallpaper" for the drumless stretches.
- **Daylight** (48) and **Afterhours** (20) next, mostly on Sampled. Far shore 25.
- **Engines:** SuperCollider 82, Sampled 78, Synth 24.
- **When:** evenings. 94 of the 184 loves came between 21:00 and 01:00, another 22 at 11:00.
- **What the loved clips share:** some breathing room in the low end. Deep field's pedal rests one
  bar in four; its loves hold 20% sub, 30% low-mid.

## What grates

Five clips; four on SuperCollider. One (10-3, at -52 dB with the volume at -30) is too quiet to read.
The other three are Daylight and Afterhours on SuperCollider (10-5 to 10-7), and they share one
thing:

| | sub % | low-mid % | centroid |
| :- | -: | -: | -: |
| SuperCollider, loved (82) | 26 | 24 | 256 Hz |
| SuperCollider, grated (3) | 42-50 | 9-12 | 148-184 Hz |

A wall below 150 Hz on every beat, burying the low-mids (the spectrogram of 10-07/221614 is solid
orange under 150 Hz, where a loved Deep field clip comes and goes). In the code: SuperCollider's bass
adds a sine an octave below every note (`utBass`, `SinOsc.ar(freq * 0.5) * 0.4`) and rings 0.4 s past
each note, so Afterhours' off-beat bass becomes a continuous sub drone under a 0.45 s kick; the Synth
engine ducks its bass under the kick, SuperCollider doesn't. Gemini on 221614 (its only critique of a
grated clip): "Fails" for all-day work, with 150-300 Hz mud from kick, sub and chords, a 2.5-4 kHz bite
on the stabs, no swing or velocity variation. The Strudel one (10-3) counted 54 clicks a minute that were
mostly its saw edges (12 by today's detector).

## Numbers that aren't opinions

- **Moods aren't level-matched:** loves run from -22.2 dB RMS (Afterhours) to -28.8 (Far shore), a
  6.6 dB spread, so a mood change is also a volume change. Far shore being the quietest may be part of
  "bland".
- **SuperCollider runs about 4 dB under** Synth and Sampled (-28.7 vs -24.5) with peaks 6-7 dB lower,
  so it's the synths' balance against the drums that's loud on it, not the whole. The new Mix slider
  (5358aa1) handles the balance; the engine gap is still there.
- **Clicks are gone since 10-5:** 0 of 88 clips on any engine. All 16 clicky clips date from 10-3 and
  10-4, before the fixes.
- **No clipping;** 56 clips peak above -6 dB, all on Synth and Sampled.

## Proposals, best evidence first

1. **SuperCollider's low end** (the grates): sub-octave down from 0.4 to about 0.12, a high-pass near
   30 Hz, the bass tail tied to the note (not +0.4 s), and the bass ducking under the kick as on Synth.
   Re-measure the three grated scenes after.
2. **Level-match the moods** to within about 1.5 dB: Far shore up about 3 dB, Afterhours down about 2.
3. **Afterhours for all day:** planned absences (the kick out for the last bar of every 8, as Deep field's
   pedal rests), softer off-beat hats, a little hat velocity jitter. Or move it off home's default.
4. **Low-mid mud:** a 2 dB dip around 250 Hz on pads and chords in every engine (open on the roadmap since
   10-3).
5. **Movement:** velocity jitter on hats and lane notes, a slow filter drift on Daylight's and
   Afterhours' pads (Far shore got one in 0.13).
6. **Bite:** soften 2-5 kHz on bells and mallets, and Deep field's bell ping on SuperCollider.
